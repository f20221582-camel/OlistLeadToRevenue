Definitions and limits

  • Valid sale: order status not canceled/unavailable. GMV: sum of item price (freight excluded), in R$.
  • Data end: order volume collapses on 2018-08-29 (Sep–Oct hold 20 orders), so the effective end is 2018-08-28.
  • Coverage: only 380 of 842 closed-deal sellers appear in the orders data; the orders file is a sample, so "not activated" is a lower bound on activation.
  • Segments, lead types and reps exist only on won deals: win rates by segment or rep cannot be computed; only post-win quality can.
  • Not experiments: channel, page and lead-type gaps are associations. Causes need a test.
  • Unusable fields: declared_monthly_revenue is positive for only 45 of 842 deals; has_company is filled for 63.
